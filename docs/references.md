# References

Literature the MomoParse Financial Health Index (MFH) sub-indexes draw from. Each formula in the index is a working version of an established personal-finance, labor-econometrics, or industrial-organization metric — listed here so anyone evaluating the score can trace the math back to its source.

| Sub-index | Formula | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Savings Rate | (Income − Expenses) / Income | Lusardi, A. & Mitchell, O. S. (2014). *The Economic Importance of Financial Literacy: Theory and Evidence.* Journal of Economic Literature, 52(1), 5–44. |
| Income Stability | σ(income) / μ(income), coefficient of variation | Gottschalk, P. & Moffitt, R. (1994). *The Growth of Earnings Instability in the U.S. Labor Market.* Brookings Papers on Economic Activity, 1994(2), 217–272. |
| Expense Volatility | σ(expenses) / μ(expenses), coefficient of variation | Morduch, J. & Schneider, R. (2017). *The Financial Diaries: How American Families Cope in a World of Uncertainty.* Princeton University Press. |
| Counterparty Concentration | Σ(shareᵢ²), Herfindahl-Hirschman Index | Hirschman, A. O. (1964). *The Paternity of an Index.* American Economic Review, 54(5), 761. |
| Transaction Velocity | transactions / days | Björkegren, D. & Grissen, D. (2018). *Behavior Revealed in Mobile Phone Usage Predicts Loan Repayment.* World Bank Economic Review. |
